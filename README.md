# ConsultChain

A decentralized business consulting marketplace connecting strategic advisors with companies for professional consulting services on Stacks blockchain.

## Features

- Consultant profile registration with specialization fields and credentials
- Consultation rate-based engagement system with direct payments
- Marketplace director accreditation for consultant quality assurance
- Engagement tracking and consulting relationship management
- Strategic business advisory through blockchain-verified consultations

## Smart Contract Functions

### Public Functions
- `register-consultant` - Register as a consultant with specialization and credentials
- `engage-consultant` - Engage a consultant for business advisory
- `accredit-consultant` - Accredit consultant credentials (director only)

### Read-Only Functions
- `get-consultant` - Get consultant profile details
- `get-engagement-record` - Get specific engagement information
- `get-engagement-count` - Get total engagements for consultant
- `get-marketplace-stats` - Get marketplace statistics

## Usage

Deploy the contract to create a decentralized consulting marketplace where business advisors can offer their expertise and companies can engage them for strategic guidance.
